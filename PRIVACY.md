# Privacy — Anxiously Okay

> **Draft for review.** Have this checked by someone qualified before you publish or submit the app.

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

Anxiously Okay is made to be private. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to what you write.

## The short version

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No servers.** The app doesn't send your entries anywhere. There is no Anxiously Okay server.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code.
- **Your data stays on your phone**, encrypted.

## What the app keeps, and where

Everything you add — moods, vents, letters, worst-case entries, wins, affirmations, notes to yourself, songs, photos, videos, drawings, voice notes, your name and reminder settings — is stored **only on your device**.

In the iPhone and Android apps it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. The key that unlocks it is created on your phone the first time you open the app, and kept in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). It never leaves your phone.

If you use Anxiously Okay in a web browser instead of the app, your entries are kept in that browser's storage on that computer, also encrypted. Browser storage is less protected than the phone apps, and your browser may clear it.

## Backups

To keep your entries on your phone only, the app's data is **not included in iCloud, iTunes/Finder, Google or device-to-device backups**. This also means:

> **If you lose or reset your phone, your entries can't be recovered.** Nobody — including us — has a copy.

## Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. Patterns (for example, which evenings have felt heavy) are worked out on your phone and never sent anywhere. Notification text on your lock screen is deliberately vague and never mentions how you've been feeling.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only if you say yes, for the reminders you choose.
- **Photos** — only when you choose photos or videos for your Comfort Room. The app only sees the items you pick.
- **Microphone** — only while you record a voice note.
- **Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint** — only if you turn on App lock. The app never sees your biometric data; your phone just tells it whether unlocking succeeded.

## Your choices

- **Export my data** (Me → Privacy): creates one file with your entries that you can save or share using your phone's share sheet. Photos, videos, drawings and voice notes are listed by reference; the files themselves stay on your phone.
- **Delete everything** (Me): permanently erases your entries, photos, videos, voice notes, drawings, scheduled reminders and the key that protects them from this phone.
- **App lock** (Me → Privacy): asks for Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your passcode when the app opens.

## Links you add

If you save a link to a song, tapping it opens that link in your browser or music app. What happens there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

## Not a replacement for care

Anxiously Okay is a supportive companion, not a medical service, and not a replacement for a therapist or doctor. If you are in crisis, the Support screen lists crisis lines you can call.

## Children

Anxiously Okay is not directed at children under 13.
